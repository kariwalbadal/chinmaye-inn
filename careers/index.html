Nº 01 — The Family

Not a job.
A family.

Since 2015, Chinmaye Inn has been another name for hospitality in Bhagalpur. And this work is not done by machines — it is done by people. By the smile at the front desk, the sizzle in Kesaria’s kitchen, the stage dressed and glowing at Maya.

So we look after our people first — a place to learn, a culture of respect, and a clear road upward. The Chinmaye Grand is nearly ready, and Deoghar arrives in 2027. Those who join today lead tomorrow.
