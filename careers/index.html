Nº 01 — The Parivaar

Kaam nahi,
parivaar.

2015 se Chinmaye Inn Bhagalpur ki mehmaan-nawazi ka doosra naam hai. Aur yeh kaam kisi machine se nahi hota — logon se hota hai. Front desk ki muskurahat se, Kesaria ke tadke se, Maya ke sajaye hue stage se.

Isliye hum pehle apne logon ka khayal rakhte hain — seekhne ka mahaul, izzat ka rishta, aur tarakki ka saaf raasta. The Chinmaye Grand taiyaar ho raha hai, Deoghar 2027 mein aa raha hai. Jo aaj join karta hai, woh kal leader banta hai.
